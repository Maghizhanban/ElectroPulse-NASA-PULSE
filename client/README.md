## 🛰️ Overview

The **ElectroPulse Flight Deck** is an edge-native clinical operations interface engineered for astronauts on deep space exploration missions (e.g., Artemis Base Camp, Mars Transit, and Surface Ops). Grounded in **NASA-STD-3001** (Volumes 1 & 2), the console synthesizes multi-modal biomedical telemetry across all 5 primary spaceflight health hazards:

1. **Space Radiation** (SPE / GCR active silicon dosimetry tracking)
2. **Isolation & Confinement** (Circadian sleep architecture & NeuroType PVT vigilance)
3. **Distance from Earth** (Complete autonomous offline execution — zero Houston reliance)
4. **Gravity Fields** (Microgravity cephalad fluid shift & SANS ocular ultrasound calipers)
5. **Hostile Closed Environments** (Cabin ECLSS partial pressure gas monitoring)

---

## ⚡ Core Client Modules

- **Medical Flight Deck (`MedicalFlightDeck.jsx`)**: Real-time mission health summary, circadian timeline, and physiological trend analytics.
- **Somatic 3D Atlas (`SomaticAtlasWorkstation.jsx`)**: Interactive cadaveric digital twin with 2,234 anatomical meshes, tissue layer slicing, and focal anomaly projection.
- **P.U.L.S.E. Autonomous Guardian (`JarvisGuardian.jsx`)**: On-board clinical AI voice assistant with reactive biometric triggers and speech synthesis.
- **PharmacoSim Drawer (`PharmacoSimDrawer.jsx`)**: Two-compartment zero-gravity pharmacokinetic drug solver predicting C_max, T_max, and clearance curves.
- **Flight Surgeon SLM View (`FlightSurgeonView.jsx`)**: Clinical Practice Guideline consultation terminal with integrated `/learn`, `/boost`, `/teamwork`, `/migrate-workflows`, `/goal`, and `/swarm` command execution.
